Walk-Forward Econometric-Graph Models for Volatility Regime Detection

When classical baselines win and where learned geometry adds value.

  • Uses walk-forward validation to cut the validation-to-test generalization gap that single-split evaluation inflates in nonstationary markets.
  • Shows EGARCH, HAR-RV, and MS-GARCH remain strong baselines for probability ranking and calibration on regime-transition prediction.
  • Finds learned geometry helps more on event-level detection and in hybrid ensembles than on raw probability ranking alone.

WIP / JEPA Theory

Geometry-Adaptive JEPA

Optimal Latent Priors Beyond the Isotropic Gaussian with Provable Convergence

  • Shows that the isotropic SIGReg prior is provably suboptimal when intrinsic dimension k is smaller than latent dimension d, explaining low-dimensional failure modes.
  • Characterizes the unique optimal prior as a structured anisotropic Gaussian supported on the encoder's invariant k-dimensional subspace.
  • Introduces MASIGReg with anti-collapse guarantees, O(k) effective complexity, and a hardware-aware description of how projections and batch size shape stochastic gradient noise.
